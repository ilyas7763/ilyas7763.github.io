How to merge PDF files into one document

Published November 19, 2026 · By the MyPDFTools team

Sending one file is easier than sending five. Merging also keeps application forms, invoices and reports together.

Before you start

Name your files in order, for example 01-cover.pdf and 02-report.pdf, so you can pick them in the right sequence.

Steps

  1. Open our Merge PDF tool.
  2. Choose your files. They are merged in the order listed.
  3. Remove any extra file, then select Merge PDF.
  4. Download and check the page order.

Is it private?

Yes. The merge happens in your browser, so your documents are not uploaded.
