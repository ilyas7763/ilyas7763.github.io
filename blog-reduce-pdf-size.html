How to reduce PDF size for email

Published November 19, 2026 · By the MyPDFTools team

Most email services stop attachments at around 20 to 25 MB, and many offices set lower limits. A large PDF usually comes from high-resolution images, so start there.

1. Check what makes it big

Scanned pages and photos are the usual cause. Text-only PDFs are rarely large.

2. Lower the image quality

Re-export the file at 150 dpi instead of 300. Text stays sharp and file size often drops by more than half.

3. Send only the pages needed

Use our Split PDF tool to keep just the pages the recipient needs.

4. Share a link instead

If the file is still too large, upload it to cloud storage and email the link.
