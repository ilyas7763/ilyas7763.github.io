How to convert photos to a PDF on your phone

Published November 19, 2026 · By the MyPDFTools team

Many offices ask for documents as one PDF, but phones save photos as JPG files. Here is the quick way to convert them.

Take clear photos

Use daylight, keep the page flat and fill the frame. Crop out the background before converting.

Convert

Open JPG to PDF, choose your photos in page order and select the button. Each image becomes one page.

Check the result

Open the PDF and confirm every page is readable before you send it.
